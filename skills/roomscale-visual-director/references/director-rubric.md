# Visual Director rubric

Score visible evidence, not prompt intent. Use 1 = failed or incoherent, 3 = recognizable but with a material weakness, and 5 = polished, clear, and production-useful. A 2 or 4 falls between the anchors. Mark categories not applicable only with a reason; do not hide a visible defect by marking it N/A.

| Category | Inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Semantic recognizability | Can a neutral viewer identify the intended object/role? |
| Silhouette | Are the major masses and negative spaces clear and distinctive? |
| Proportions | Do major parts relate plausibly and match known dimensions/source evidence? |
| Construction believability | Are components connected, load paths and joins plausible, and parts manufactured consistently? |
| Geometric/detail richness | Are useful medium-scale forms present, beyond tiny surface noise? |
| Material realism | Do glass, fabric, wood, metal, stone, or organic materials have the right visible response? |
| Surface variation | Are broad surfaces controlled and distinct without noise or repetition? |
| Art-direction fit | Does the image fit Human Room, Clockwork, Verdant, or Scavenger references? |
| Scale cues | Are proportions legible against the 0.5-inch citizen or an accurately dimensioned scale view? |
| Gameplay-distance readability | Will the defining identity survive the intended RoomScale camera distance? |
| Source resemblance | For a photographed object, does it preserve its visible character? |
| 3D-reference transferability | Can a modeler infer a coherent silhouette, construction, and material assignment? |

Answer all four questions for the actual image/reference pack:

- **Professional visual read:** Would an ordinary viewer call this polished concept/reference art instead of an obvious AI draft?
- **Structural coherence:** Are major parts connected, physically plausible, and consistently designed?
- **Detail hierarchy:** Are important medium-scale forms present rather than replaced by tiny decorative noise?
- **3D transferability:** Could a 3D worker infer coherent geometry from this evidence?

Call out malformed geometry, impossible joins, inconsistent perspective, disconnected components, AI artifacts, and visually nonsensical details explicitly. A scorecard does not erase a hard failure: record the defect and its location/view.
