# Batch 22 — Scavenger Depot Gameplay-Scale Review

## Scope and decision

This batch created three new Blender-authored, presentation-only Scavenger Depot candidates with PBR materials. The Luna-approved gantry target was transferred through the three allowed Visual Director strategies. The actual Room A scene was used for the final comparison so the 64-inch camera showed the Depot among the settlement buildings.

The first candidate was rebuilt twice. v002 enlarged the appliance panels and reduced front framing. v003 changed to an open-front gantry with stronger blue and cream color blocks, a recessed roof, and a broad center route. Godot 4.7.2 validation passed at 23.63 × 12.18 × 18.61 inches, with 10 meshes/surfaces and 52,048 triangles. The asset remains presentation-only; no gameplay or settlement contracts changed.

Luna's corrected Room A review scores v003's 64-inch first read 4/5, its target correspondence 4/5, door and route readability 4/5, and Scavenger identity 4/5. It is the first candidate whose paired appliance doors and open route read clearly in the actual settlement view. The hoist and side storage remain too small to read at gameplay distance (2/5), the roof is more regular than the target (3/5), and close craft is below the production bar (3/5). The episode is therefore backlogged after attempt three. v003 remains the best current review candidate; no candidate was promoted.

The initial v001/v002 reviews used the default `room_photo_luna` fixture, whose settlement-scale camera did not center the Depot. Those reviews are preserved as historical evidence. The corrected comparison uses the Room A fixture and supersedes their gameplay-scale conclusions.

## Disposition

- Attempts: `scavenger_depot-v001`, `v002`, and `v003`.
- Best current 3D review candidate: v003.
- Accepted production asset: none; the canonical registry remains unchanged.
- Promotion: none. The existing settlement presentation remains in production.
- Target episode: backlogged after attempt three because the hoist and side storage remain unreadable at gameplay scale and close craft is below target.
- Follow-up if this asset returns under a new approved target: enlarge the plain rope hoist and one side storage form while preserving the clear center route and paired doors.
- Next priority: continue the Scavenger settlement architecture family with the next high-prominence structure; do not add a fourth candidate to this Depot target episode.
