# Batch 23 — Scavenger Housing Gameplay-Scale Review

## Scope and disposition

This batch created three Scavenger Housing 3D candidates from a Luna-reviewed concept direction and refined target. All models are presentation-only; the housing landmark, citizen assignments, collision, navigation and simulation remain unchanged.

The key comparison uses the actual Room A scene at the 64-inch settlement camera. Luna scores v003's gameplay readability 4/5: the high canopy and lower set-back lean-to separate the two homes, and the blue/cream doors remain visible. The close image looks stronger than the room view; fine wear and construction detail are less visible at 64 inches. This is a static production-path Room A render, not an interactive play capture.

v002 initially read as one duplex-like mass and exceeded the width envelope at 17.272 inches. v003 separated the roof geometry and fits the brief at 16.66 × 6.515 × 8.848 inches. It contains one mesh object, nine material surfaces, and 38,068 triangles. v004's alternate tile-detail PBR adds subtle close-range variation but does not improve geometric detail (3/5), materials (3/5), surface variation (3/5), close-up quality (3/5), or semantic recognition (4/5) to the promotion thresholds.

The approved target episode is backlogged after its three candidates. v003 remains the best current gameplay-read candidate; v004 remains review-only. No candidate was promoted, and the accepted registry remains unchanged.
