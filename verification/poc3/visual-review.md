# Codex visual review — 2026-10-01

Reviewed all 18 canonical final screenshots at 1280×720, plus baseline room,
settlement, and completed grapple images. This is direct image inspection by
Codex, not human acceptance or a live animation review.

Verdict: meaningful visual improvement, but I would not approve AC-43 yet.
Citizen close views and the grapple establish a recognizable stylized direction;
the overall settlement still reads as a sparse procedural prototype.

| Area | Observation | Follow-up priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Citizens | Hats, goggles, boots, shoulder plates, belts and colored shirts make a consistent, recognizable silhouette. Close views read clearly. Role differences are less obvious from the front. | Medium: stronger role silhouettes and pose variety. |
| Resources | The plank bundle and metal cargo distinguish actual hauling clearly in resource-carry-close. | Preserve this direction. |
| Furniture | Books, cushions, drawer fronts and pulls improve recognition over baseline primitives. Room-scale furniture still lacks a unified finished art treatment. | Medium: consistent edge, texture and prop treatment. |
| Settlement | Roof seams, warm windows, framing and chimney add detail. Large empty floor regions, isolated benches and tiny citizens weaken the sense of a connected settlement. | High: improve composition and visual connection between activity areas while preserving gameplay scale. |
| Materials | Wood grain supplies detail, but its stretched high-contrast bands dominate floor-level frames. Some yellow/brass machinery faces appear flat and bright. | High: reduce floor contrast and tune texture scale; improve material separation. |
| Lighting / builder | Sunlit citizens read well. The builder beneath the desk is dark, and the screenshot does not clearly demonstrate the hammer or a building pose. | High: improve shadow readability and capture a visible working pose. |
| Grapple | Spool wraps, gears, rails, ladder and pulley are a substantial improvement over baseline. The diagonal cable route is readable in the close climb image. | Medium: refine metal highlights and the hand/cable relationship. |
| Capture framing | citizen-traversal-detail shows mostly empty desktop; elevated-surface-exploration-detail leaves the citizen very small. Several wide captures serve state evidence rather than visual quality review. | High: retain context captures, but improve subject tracking and framing for detail captures. |
| UI | The large diagnostic panel covers much of the room in overview/state captures. UI-free close captures are more useful for art review. | Medium: add a compact presentation HUD for normal viewing. |

Static images cannot verify smooth animation or temporal aliasing. Existing
gameplay test results remain valid functional evidence, but do not resolve
these visual findings. No rendering or gameplay code was changed during this
review. AC-43 remains open for human review as required by the project plan.
