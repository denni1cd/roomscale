## Exact amendment

For `desk` and `citizen` only, the previous fixed challenger count is no longer a stopping condition. Continue autonomous experimentation while a technically credible materially different strategy remains, including reasonable refinement cycles where evidence shows progress. Do not stop merely because the former eight-challenger budget was exhausted.

The Art Factory's pilot brief/configuration must represent this as **no numeric attempt cap**, rather than an arbitrarily large replacement number. This changes the permitted number of attempts only; candidate generation, technical validation, production-path rendering, visual evaluation, gameplay checks, incumbent comparison, promotion, and rejection rules remain those of the approved contract.

Preserve every existing candidate, evaluation, render, history, and current best-so-far incumbent. `desk-v007` and `citizen-v007` remain incumbents until a challenger passes every existing gate and genuinely beats them. Existing `desk-v009` evidence remains a failed pre-amendment challenger and is not erased or rewritten as a post-amendment strategy.

## Plateau stop condition

A pilot may be declared technically plateaued only after at least three materially different credible production strategies have been attempted **after this amendment**. Each must have production-path RoomScale renders and reasonable refinements; the same fundamental quality limitation must still prevent the unchanged visual gate from passing; and the evidence must name the missing capability or pipeline component. A plateau is `BLOCKED`, never `PASS`. If a credible strategy remains, experimentation continues.

The pre-amendment `desk-v009` attempt does not count toward the three-strategy post-amendment threshold. The first post-amendment Desk and Citizen challengers retain their normal next candidate IDs in their histories.

## Unchanged binding requirements

No other acceptance criterion, visual threshold, rubric, evidence requirement, anti-placeholder gate, gameplay invariant, RoomDefinition boundary, runtime-independence rule, migration gate, performance criterion, or completion rule changes. No incumbent is deleted, regressed, or promoted without the unchanged complete gate. Broad primary-asset migration and rendering completion remain downstream of all three canonical pilots passing.

The new user-provided continuation prompt itself is the user's explicit authorization for this exact narrow amendment; no new user decision is requested. The independent Rules Lawyer is asked only to confirm the amendment is bounded to the authorized attempt-limit change and introduces no weakening or unrelated scope.
