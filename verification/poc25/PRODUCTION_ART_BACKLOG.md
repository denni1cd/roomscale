# RoomScale Production-Art Backlog

This backlog is derived from actual strict-gate and production-scene evidence. These items do not block the amended Art Factory POC on their own. They remain necessary before the corresponding visuals can be called production-final. None of the entries below changes historical PASS/FAIL decisions.
