# RoomScale POC 2.5 — Closure and Next-Phase Handoff

## POC 2.5 delivered

POC 2.5 delivered a reusable autonomous Art Factory that preserves:

- `ArtBrief` and `ArtRecipe` contracts;
- replaceable backend/tool workers and recorded inputs, versions, seeds, methods, and outputs;
- immutable candidate/provenance history and incumbent/challenger comparisons;
- technical validation and stable semantic asset resolution;
- actual Godot production-path rendering, visual inspection, diagnosis, and refinement;
- evidence-backed evaluation, honest rejection, and explicit failure visibility;
- best-so-far preservation and reversible promotion;
- explicit review-only candidate integration;
- separation between presentation assets and authoritative RoomDefinition/gameplay geometry;
- local project assets at runtime, with no AI-service dependency.

The factory exercised procedural/Godot and Blender workflows, image-conditioned 3D backends including TRELLIS.2 with its intended DINOv3 weights, Pixal3D, Hunyuan3D 2.1, RecGen3D, hosted SUPRA-Tex, MPFB2, and scripted/material-authoring paths. The final showcase uses citizen-v089, desk-v007, and grapple-v006 only through the review-only registry. The [v089 gameplay evidence](final/v089-showcase-smoke-summary.json) confirms the three final-room regressions without changing the canonical registry.

## POC 2.5 does not claim

- production-quality art across all of RoomScale;
- that every pilot passes its unchanged strict visual gate;
- a completed room-wide visual overhaul;
- a permanent commitment to TRELLIS.2, Pixal3D, Hunyuan3D, MPFB2, Blender, or any other backend.

The matched comparison's finding remains: broad scene-wide transformation has not yet occurred. Under [the final criterion-8 amendment](POC25_ACCEPTANCE_AMENDMENT_2026-10-29.md), that is a next-phase production finding, not a prerequisite for closing the Art Factory POC.

## Next phase: Art Factory production deployment

The next phase uses the Art Factory to improve RoomScale broadly. It should:

1. inventory essentially all visible RoomScale art;
2. assign stable semantic IDs where needed;
3. classify assets by visual importance and current quality;
4. process prioritized batches through the Art Factory;
5. use bounded effort per asset so one difficult object cannot block the project;
6. periodically evaluate the whole integrated game;
7. move unresolved quality issues into the existing production-art backlog;
8. replace generation backends when stronger supported tools become available.

No production migration or new pilot iteration is included in POC 2.5 closure.
