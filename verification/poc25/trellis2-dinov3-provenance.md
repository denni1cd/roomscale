**Status (2026-10-28):** v031/v032 prove successful TRELLIS.2 inference with its intended DINOv3 conditioner and a RoomScale production-render test, but their synthetic conditioning image does not constitute a direct source-photo fidelity test. The direct-photo v048 TRELLIS.2 inference used the intended DINOv3 checkpoint and completed; v049 repaired its packaging and passed technical validation, but the actual RoomScale output fails the strict visual gate. Preserve v007 and both challenger histories. Pixal3D v050 is a separate Art Factory strategy, not a replacement encoder inside TRELLIS.2.

## Input-provenance correction and direct-photo rerun

- Although the v031 recipe names `photo_holder/Sample (2).jpg` as its acceptance photo, `CONDITIONING_IMAGE` in `scripts/art_factory/generators/trellis2_desk_v031.py` actually points to `art/desk/source_views/hunyuan21-clean-reference-cutout-v026.png`. That image is a synthetic studio-style render. The v031/v032 runs therefore remain valid inference/package/render evidence, but cannot be cited as source-photograph-conditioned fidelity evidence.
- The desk-v048 source view is a direct crop of `photo_holder/Sample (2).jpg`, recorded in `art/desk/source_views/desk-photo-crop-v048.provenance.json`. No redraw or object replacement is used. `scripts/art_factory/generators/trellis2_desk_v048.py` pins TRELLIS.2's existing DINOv3 conditioner to the official `facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-lvd1689m` snapshot at `ea8dc2863c51be0a264bab82070e3e8836b02d51`; the generator records the configuration and weight hashes. This is the trained encoder expected by TRELLIS.2, not an encoder substitution.
- `desk-v048` completed TRELLIS.2 inference from that direct crop and produced a GLB. Its recipe listed a PNG before `desk.tscn`, so the first Art Factory validation inspected the wrong primary output; preserve this packaging failure. `desk-v049` re-packaged the same generated geometry with the scene first, passes Godot validation at 66 × 30 × 30 inches with 291,423 triangles, and has all six standardized RoomScale captures. The actual render is a dark fused mass with no clearly resolved standing-desk assembly, so the strict visual gate fails. It is model and production evidence, not acceptance.
- No newer encoder was substituted inside TRELLIS.2. The separately pinned Pixal3D DINOv3 projection conditioner in v050 belongs to Pixal3D's own official single-image pipeline and is recorded in `art/desk/recipes/v050.json`.

The DINOv3 snapshot revision/hash above was recovered from the local HF cache after the v031 inference. The v031 record pins the DINOv3 repository ID but not its commit in the generation manifest; the pre-run cache timestamp plus completed inference and exact output hash link that run to the intended checkpoint. The fresh v048 run records its revision and hashes directly. Keep TRELLIS.2/DINOv3 weights and generated model outputs local pending publication/license review.
