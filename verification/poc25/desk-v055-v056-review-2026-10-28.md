## Method and controlled change

Both challengers keep the authored v042 desk geometry unchanged and use Hunyuan3D 2.1 PaintPBR only on the isolated `SmokedGlass_PBR` surface. The unchanged GLB geometry hash is `180961b509c46cb7479af4edb8ae8af97f9fe2a4ef76dc23785d581455971515` in both generation records. Graphite, rubber, steel, dimensions, RoomDefinition, and gameplay geometry were not edited.

## Next step and method feasibility

Do not run another paint-only or shader-only variant. The next Desk attempt must materially change geometry fidelity and assembly construction, or use a source-conditioned geometry refiner that runs in an approved environment.

The newly released official [Hi3D v1.0 installation guide](https://github.com/hi3d-official/Hi3Dv1.0/blob/main/docs/installation.md) describes a promising coarse-mesh-plus-reference-image geometry refinement route, but requires Linux, Python 3.10, and a matching CUDA Toolkit 12.0–12.5 build. It reports only 96 GB H20 validation for its 1536³ ladder and does not establish feasibility on this 32 GB RTX 5090. This Windows host has WSL2 virtualization disabled and only CUDA Toolkit 13.0; do not count Hi3D as an inference trial or claim an output result.

[SUPRA-Tex's official repository](https://github.com/Supra-3D/supra-tex) is another possible material strategy, but it requires Linux and CUDA 12.4 with native extensions; its material adapter also has a separate research license. It is not a substitute for a geometry-changing Desk attempt and is not run on this host.

These host limitations do not establish a Desk plateau while a credible compatible production method remains. All generated PaintPBR outputs remain local under the existing model-license handling.
