## Layout estimates and ambiguity

The four photos do not provide a full overhead or single complete perimeter view. The two windows appear to meet around a corner in Sample (1), and a window and glazed double doors appear on adjoining walls in Sample (4). The model therefore places one window on each of the south and west walls and double doors on the east wall, with the workstation and dragon art against the north wall. Exact wall identities, window offsets, door location, room proportions, and furniture spacing remain estimates. The map, clock, scrollwork, and wall emblem placements are approximate. A photo from the room center aimed level at each of the four walls would resolve the perimeter and opening positions; a measured wall length would improve scale.

The hammock is a fabric sling on a separate-looking support frame, but the JSON uses one broad approximate obstacle because the generic shape cannot represent its sagging fabric and narrow crossed supports. Artwork and wall ornaments are reduced to thin, flat boxes. The photos show outside views through the windows; the generic shell can represent window panes but not the porch, landscape, figures, collectibles, screens, cables, bedding piles, or loose floor clutter. The ceiling appears turquoise and has visible wooden crown molding; the ceiling is modeled as flat because the photos do not establish its shape.
