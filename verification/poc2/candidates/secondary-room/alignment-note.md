# Second-room visual review

The saved render has separate views of the fireplace/built-ins, coffee table, shaded windows/sliding door, and sofa/opposite wall. The central object inventory, wall adjacency, carpet, major window groupings, hearth, and table are visually identifiable.

The render still differs materially from the source photos. Its fireplace stones and bookcase contents are block-based; the tabletop has no photographic marble texture; the sofa, trim, cushions, and glass are simplified; the dragon artwork is a colored geometric motif rather than the pictured painting; and the shell is only an approximate perimeter. The actual photo set includes a full-range of light, reflection, texture, and perspective that this procedural renderer does not reproduce.

For that reason the cross-room result is recorded as a validated, rendered candidate—not as a photo-faithful reconstruction, complete gameplay proof, or accepted demonstration of AC-18/19. The user's concern about close visual alignment remains valid.
