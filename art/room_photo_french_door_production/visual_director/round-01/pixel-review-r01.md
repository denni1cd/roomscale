# Pixel review — French door target r01

**Decision: approve as the separate Visual Director target for this bounded transfer attempt.**

- The full paired-door silhouette, casing crown, both leaves, and threshold are visible with no cropped edges.
- The target reads as a constructed residential French-door assembly. Each leaf has a clear central meeting relationship, tall glazing, true stiles and rails, and a two-column divided-light grid with about five rows.
- Reddish warm-stained timber and grain correspond to the primary CC BY 4.0 source image. The original RoomScale photo is used only for the matching dark warm trim and green room context.
- Garden color is visible through the glazing; the panes do not read as black or opaque. The lower leaves retain a substantial rail without an oversized opaque panel.
- Three outer hinges per leaf and central lever/latch hardware are visibly separated from the wood and large enough to guide the close-view model.
- The target includes a subdued green wall and timber floor in its installed context. It contains no furniture or unrelated openings and keeps the door as the dominant subject.

**Limits carried into 3D review:** the external photograph does not resolve all hardware and section details. The concept's hardware is a restrained construction cue, not a claim about the exact source door. Candidate validation must still confirm the exact 66 × 82 × 4 inch envelope, readable hardware and glazing in the production close view, and unchanged RoomDefinition/gameplay state.
