- The warm reddish oak has visible, board-aligned grain. Rails, muntins, meeting stile, casing and threshold show layered depth.
- The glass remains transparent with restrained reflections; leafy garden and pale paving are visible beyond it. All foliage appears outside the door.
- The interior is limited to a deep-green wall, matching timber base trim and warm floor. No furniture or unrelated decor crowds the opening.
- The timber appears somewhat brighter than the prior target, but remains within the brief's warm-stained oak direction. The oblique camera is the intended material change: it makes hardware and fitted depth assessable without changing the required door configuration.
