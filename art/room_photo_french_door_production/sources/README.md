# French-door source reference

Primary door reference: **Michael Gaylard, “Interior of a room with ornate antique furniture and decorative tiling. (55253834004)”**, via Wikimedia Commons. The photograph shows a reddish warm-stained timber pair with divided glazing and a visible meeting stile. It is licensed under **CC BY 4.0**; attribution and source links are recorded in `source-attribution.json`.

The included image is an unmodified 3840 × 2560 Commons scaled file. The source photo supplied with RoomScale shows room windows and trim but no door. Use it to match the room’s warm timber and context, and use the Commons photo for the door design. The door photo does not resolve hinge and latch construction clearly, so hardware should remain simple and plausible without claiming a photo match.

The third Visual Director transfer uses `ai-generated-stained-oak-albedo-r01.png`, an AI-generated texture made from the separately approved door target. It is not a crop of the Commons photograph. Keep the CC BY 4.0 attribution above when distributing the derived target or texture; detailed provenance is in `source-attribution.json`.


The round-02 French-door target uses a separate r03 stained-oak albedo input. It is lower contrast and less red than r01, with per-member UV phase/scale variation in the candidate recipe. It remains a candidate material until Godot close and room views confirm seams and natural scale; see `ai-generated-stained-oak-albedo-r03-review.md`.
