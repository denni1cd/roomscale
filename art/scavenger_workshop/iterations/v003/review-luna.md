## Findings

The close views show a real improvement over v002: the front work bay is more exposed, and its rear tool wall, shelves, and bench are easier to read without the roof dominating as much of the opening. The effect is most visible in the 27.7-inch three-quarter view. It is a useful improvement to the asset's intended work function.

At the matched 64-inch settlement framing, the workshop remains small and roof-heavy. The bench and open bay are discernible, but the small tool details do not resolve clearly enough to call this a material gameplay-readability jump over v002. At 330 inches in the room overview, the asset is too small for its internal bay layout to register; v002 and v003 occupy effectively the same visual footprint there.

The roof still reads as supported: its front edge sits over a continuous beam carried by corner posts, with diagonal braces at the sides. The shortened roof span preserves the lean-to silhouette. The asset retains Scavenger cues through the mismatched appliance doors, sheet-metal roof, timber framing, and improvised storage. A bright cog remains visible inside and adds some Clockwork association, but it does not dominate the overall identity.

The proportions are plausible and now technically confirmed within the intended roughly 26 × 20 × 20-inch envelope. In engine, the simplified geometry and flat presentation remain visibly below the polished approved concept's material richness, although they are coherent enough for a gameplay asset review.
