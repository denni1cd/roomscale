# Scavenger Workshop Visual Director — r01

## Direction exploration

The generated [three-direction sheet](candidates/directions-r01.png) was reviewed at full size by Luna. The selected direction is the center concept: an open-front lean-to assembled around a salvaged appliance door, mismatched sheet panels, drawer parts, wire basket, timber frame, and workbench.

| Direction | Pixel review | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left — drawer platform and canvas roof | Sturdy and readable, but the tall drawer carcass, raised deck, and stairs make it bulky. | Keep as an alternate; do not transfer. |
| Center — appliance-panel lean-to | Strong compact silhouette; domestic appliance parts read clearly; open front, bench, and shelves remain usable. Roof-edge attachment needs clearer support, and the large enamel door should not block the opening. | Develop this direction. |
| Right — wire-rack cage and canvas | Most distinctive scaffold shape, but over-scaffolded for a small workshop and the fabric obscures the work area. | Keep as an alternate; do not transfer. |

Luna's review also flagged faction overlap: all three directions share warm lanterns, rusted metal, timber, and dense tool walls. Refine the selected direction around chipped domestic enamel, appliance handles, drawer joinery, ordinary screws and straps, and uneven sheet overlaps. Do not use brass or gears as the primary identity cue. Keep the roof attachment and salvage load paths explicit, and maintain a clear view into the work area.

## Selected target handoff

The first single-image refinement preserved the appliance-panel lean-to but left a large fan/motor and brass-like pipe fittings that overlapped Clockwork identity. Luna requested removing those features and simplifying the workbench clutter. The second refinement made the work area clearer and removed those mechanical cues, but Luna requested shortening the unsupported right overhang. The final image shortens the roof so the edge lands above the side frame.

## Approved target — center refined r03

Luna pixel-inspected [center-refined-r03](candidates/center-refined-r03.png) and approved it for 3D handoff. The compact appliance-panel lean-to reads as Scavenger salvage; its open work face, bench, shelves, timber posts, diagonal braces, roof overlaps, and supported feet remain visible. The shortened right roof edge now ends over the side frame. The 26 × 20 × 20-inch envelope is plausible from the concept view but remains a 3D validation requirement; the image itself does not prove exact dimensions.

Approval is limited to the concept target. It does not approve a 3D candidate or change the production registry. Future candidate evaluation must test the open face, structural joins, salvage-vs-Clockwork identity, and readability at gameplay distance.
