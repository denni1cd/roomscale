# Lighting and Environment Visual Target — Round 01

Pixel review by Luna of the generated 1280 × 720 Room B overview concept.

Approved as a direction reference for: lower ambient wash, controlled pale-surface exposure, warm directional daylight, softer grounded shadows, warm existing settlement light, and a quieter cool exterior.

The image generator also introduced apparent wall/floor texture and a window-like shadow grid that are not supported by the input scene. Those details are explicitly excluded. The target does not authorize geometry, material, texture, object, or gameplay changes. Implementation must change only existing light and environment settings, and the matched Room A/B/photo-room renders remain the production evidence.
