The 330-inch room views show no useful difference between v001 and v002, which is expected given the asset's small screen footprint at that distance.

## Decision — alternate strategy

Do not pass v002. The requested local changes improve the close view slightly but do not fix the first read in the 64-inch crop, and the central Clockwork-coded object remains. Further edits to post placement, slab size, or hoist detail alone are unlikely to bridge the silhouette and faction-identity gap.

Rebuild around one broad open loading face under a visibly asymmetric single roof. Put the large blue and cream fixed appliance panels at the two outer sides of that face, make the center route unmistakably empty, and suspend a plain rope-and-wood hoist from the front crossbeam with that beam carried by corner posts and diagonal braces. Remove the gold-banded cylinder and gear-like detail. Keep roof seams, supports, enamel blocks, and galvanized patches broad enough to read at 64 inches. The next candidate should be judged on its settlement capture before close-range microdetail is added.
