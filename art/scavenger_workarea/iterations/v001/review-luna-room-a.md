# Scavenger Work Area v001 — Luna Room A Review

## Decision

**Targeted correction.** The actual Room A 64-inch views show the broad roof as the first read. The bench, vise, and blue tool board are obscured, so the structure reads more like a generic open shelter than a hand-work area and remains too similar to the Depot's roofed frame. The close view confirms the posts and roof beams but does not restore the work function.

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---:|
| Work-area identity | 2/5 |
| Bench/tool-board visibility | 1/5 |
| Open entrance | 3/5 |
| Material read | 3/5 |
| Support plausibility | 3/5 |
| Target transfer | 2/5 |

Shorten the camera-facing roof depth and overhang by about one third, and raise the front eave while remaining under the 9-inch height limit. Keep the blue/cream tool board and large tools visible above the bench from the Room A camera. Preserve the open entrance, timber supports, and all simulation-owned work-site behavior.
