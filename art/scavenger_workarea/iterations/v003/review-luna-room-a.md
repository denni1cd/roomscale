# Scavenger Work Area v003 — Luna Room A Review

## Decision

**Backlog this target episode.** At the controlled Room A 64-inch camera proxy, the broad bright roof dominates; the bench reads as a pale horizontal band, the vise is too small to identify, and the blue/cream tool board and large tools are not legible. The structure reads more like a canopy shelter than a hand-work area. The close proxy confirms the roof and bench are present, but does not restore the work function. These captures use the Godot production scene and controlled Room A camera; they are not live interactive gameplay footage.

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---:|
| Work-area identity | 1/5 |
| Bench and vise visibility | 2/5 |
| Tool-board and large-tool visibility | 1/5 |
| Open entrance | 3/5 |
| Material separation | 2/5 |
| Supports and load path | 3/5 |
| Technical envelope fit | 5/5 |

The candidate technically validates at 22.81 × 8.045 × 10.03 inches. The solid material blocks produce a pale white/salmon roof and pale bench that do not separate well in the Room A render. If this target is reopened later, shift or reduce the camera-facing canopy and make the tool board, separated large tools, bench and vise the first read beneath a visibly supported roof.
