# Scavenger Work Area — Visual Development Round 01

## Brief

Create one open hand-work bay in the existing 23 × 9 × 12 inch presentation envelope. At the 64-inch Room A camera, the bench, a broad tool board and open entrance must read before small tools. Preserve work-site behavior and keep the structure distinct from the Scavenger Depot and paired housing.

## Direction exploration

`candidates/directions-triptych.png` compares three open-bay directions. Luna selected Direction C (right) for its clearest hand-work function, separated from the Depot and housing by its bench, vise, blue tool board and open entrance. Direction C's first roof was too tall and roof-heavy for the envelope, so the concept was refined with a lower canopy, one outer canvas patch, and no second work station.

## Refined target

`candidates/direction-c-refined-r01.png` isolates the lower-roof Direction C. It retains a wide bench, one hand vise, one blue tool board with spaced large tools, visible timber braces, a single outer canvas patch, and a small crate. Luna's final pixel review and target disposition are recorded in `pixel-review.md` before 3D generation.

The concept sheet is a thumbnail-scale proxy. The 64-inch Room A production render, not the concept, will determine whether the work bay reads during gameplay.
