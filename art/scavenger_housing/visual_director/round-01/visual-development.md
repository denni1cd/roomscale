# Scavenger Housing — Visual Development Round 01

## Brief

Build two distinct compact Scavenger homes within the existing 17 × 7 × 9 inch presentation envelope. The broad blue and cream salvaged appliance doors, different low roof shapes, repaired timber, small windows, and one shared landing must read before fine trim. Keep the model presentation-only; preserve room landmarks, citizen housing logic, and assignments.

## Direction exploration

`candidates/directions-triptych.png` compares three paired-cottage directions. Luna selected the center panel, Direction B, for clearer separation at thumbnail scale through its roof profiles and small gap. The concept review explicitly treated this as an estimate, not as a gameplay capture.

## Target refinement and review

The refinement isolates Direction B, joins the porches, removes extra roof uprights, and retains visible braces and one plain vent. Luna approved the refined target for 3D handoff. See `pixel-review.md` for scores and limits.

The approved target establishes form and material direction only. The first 3D candidate will be judged in the 64-inch camera in `rooms/room_a.json`; close renders will diagnose material and construction transfer, not determine gameplay success.
