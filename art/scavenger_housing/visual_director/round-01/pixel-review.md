## Refined target

Luna approved `candidates/direction-b-refined-r01.png` for 3D handoff after a targeted revision. The pair remains distinguishable through its different roof shapes, shared landing, and broad blue and cream doors. Timber posts, braces, and porch supports show plausible load paths. Reclaimed wood, canvas, and sheet metal establish Scavenger identity; the single plain capped vent has no Clockwork cues.
