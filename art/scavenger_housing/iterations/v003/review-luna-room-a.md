# Scavenger Housing v003 — Luna Room A Review

## Decision

**Target transfer: pass.** v003 is a clear improvement over v002 in the matched Room A 64-inch views. The lowered right roof separates the cottage silhouettes, while the blue and cream doors still read around the shared porch. Opposite and side views preserve that separation; the close view confirms visible framing beneath both roofs.

The transfer from the target is strong: two homes, contrasting roof profiles, broad appliance-door colors, and reclaimed materials survive. Timber posts, beams, and braces make the construction plausible. The homes show no Clockwork cues. The 16.66 × 6.515 × 8.848 inch bounds fit the 17 × 7 × 9 inch envelope. Joining authored geometry into one mesh with nine material surfaces reduces fragmentation while retaining the visual material groups.
