# Verdant Nursery target revision — round 02

The first concept was visually appealing but did not constrain its apparent vertical scale to the existing Room A workshop envelope. Blender candidate `verdant_nursery-v001` measured 23.26 × 9.44 × 10.52 inches against 26 × 10 × 18 inches, and the Art Factory technical gate rejected its 58.5% front-to-back depth ratio. The Visual Director episode was backlogged before a production render; no gameplay-scale image was made from v001.

Round 02 retains Direction C's asymmetrical cultivated-tree identity and work platform while representing the actual low, broad room module. The 3D build must target Godot X=26 inches width, Y=10 inches height, Z=18 inches depth, and keep the front work bay and entry approach visible. The approval is for the target image only. Godot validation and 64-inch Room A production captures remain required.
