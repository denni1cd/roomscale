# Verdant Growth Nursery — Visual Development r01

## Target brief

Create a Tier 2 faction-defining Growth Nursery model within the Room A workshop's 26 × 10 × 18 inch presentation site. Preserve the existing authoritative workshop node, construction state, nursery work behavior, navigation, collision and assignments. The primary question is whether a living-tree silhouette and distinct layered foliage read at the 64-inch settlement camera while the front work bay stays open.

## Image development

Built-in ImageGen generated a three-column concept triptych showing four related Verdant structure roles. Luna inspected the pixels and chose Direction C (right column) for its plum-and-green foliage identity and clear role separation. The triptych provides family context; its other structures are not included in this Growth Nursery candidate.

The initial refined C nursery image was rejected after Luna found the canopy tiers and root fan crowded the work bay. One targeted edit compacted them inward and cleared the front platform. Luna approved the corrected image as the r01 visual target.

## Approved target and transfer

The final target keeps the braided trunk, plum/green layered foliage, seed pods and nursery platform, with a visibly open front. Its canvas is a concept proxy only. The first 3D candidate must be checked against the 26 × 10 × 18 inch Godot bounds and reviewed in the controlled Room A 64-inch settlement camera before close detail can count as a gameplay-read improvement.
